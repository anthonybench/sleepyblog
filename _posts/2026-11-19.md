## Surprise post after exactly one year!

I did it, I actually came back to my blog, updated it, and wrote another post! \
I wasn't entirely sure I would, so....hooray 🤣 more content for all one people that reads this (hi Mike).

Today marks the anniversary of the day we passed inspection and proposal (well technically it was Tuesday, but this is one year since the last post so close enough 😎). We've built and improved so much in that year, including (but not supported by pictures because i never remember to take them):

- a 5' sidewalk that wraps around the whole house, holding back ground water and pests from our foundation
- a super awesome custom bomb-proof hammock stand (i want more of them)
- started a massive project involving tearing down a camper to it's chassis, cleaning it up, repairing tires, painting it, welding stabilizer plates and a jack on it, and eventually building a cover over it and decking it for an outdoor movable patio
- cleaned up a ton in the wake of the construction, and now our gravel is fairly orderly
- a ton of clearing
- a cart to carry all my kayaks and keep them off the ground
- a cart to hold tools outside all year round

And currently we're clearing space to build a big pole barn and 2 other insulated sheds for more storage and work infrastructure. The plan is that by the dry months in 2027, we'll be ready to get started with farming/gardening, and have a lot more managed space for social antics.

And we got to travel around Japan for a couple weeks with 3 other friends, such a great vacation!

More than just a land update, this post is meant to celebrate to my partner Jean. Without her, none of this would have been possible, she's more than amazing ❤️
